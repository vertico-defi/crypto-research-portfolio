# LinkedIn post drafts

All drafts are educational and contain no buy or sell recommendation.

## 1. A 20% backtest is not automatically a strategy
**Verified fact:** The public adapter does not contain the requested v5 immutable artifact.\
**Interpretation:** A number without its protocol, costs, and audit context is incomplete evidence.\
**Opinion:** Research should publish the gate before the headline.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Independent audit and sealed holdout status for that unavailable artifact.

## 2. Why we refused to open the holdout
**Verified fact:** The published Strategy Control snapshot says final holdouts remain sealed, unopened, and unread.\
**Interpretation:** That preserves an independent decision boundary.\
**Opinion:** A holdout is worth more closed than repurposed for another iteration.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Whether a future preregistered gate will justify opening it.

## 3. Passing hundreds of tests did not prove profitability
**Verified fact:** The portfolio distinguishes implementation validation from economic evaluation.\
**Interpretation:** Tests support software-contract claims, not market-edge claims.\
**Opinion:** Teams should state what each test cannot establish.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Net profitability under an approved evaluation protocol.

## 4. Implementation failure is not strategy failure
**Verified fact:** Published routes include implementation-inconclusive outcomes with no economic result.\
**Interpretation:** An unverified implementation does not prove the hypothesis true or false.\
**Opinion:** “Inconclusive” is more useful than pretending a system was tested.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Whether an independent implementation can satisfy the protocol.

## 5. Why doubled trading costs matter
**Verified fact:** The public record identifies absent executable bid/ask and unfrozen fees/slippage as limits.\
**Interpretation:** Cost sensitivity can change the meaning of a gross result.\
**Opinion:** A research report should make cost stress tests routine.\
**Unresolved evidence:** The future net result under frozen executable costs.

## 6. Why an audit can stop an attractive result
**Verified fact:** Perp Carry recorded complete snapshots but failed its frozen timing gate.\
**Interpretation:** Collection completeness alone cannot establish reliable timing.\
**Opinion:** An audit should be allowed to veto a convenient narrative.\
**Unresolved evidence:** A compliant repeat audit and any profitability evidence.

## 7. What survivorship bias does to crypto research
**Verified fact:** The methodology requires point-in-time construction.\
**Interpretation:** Today’s available assets can distort an earlier choice set.\
**Opinion:** Universe definitions belong in preregistration, not an appendix.\
**Unresolved evidence:** The effect size for any individual unreported strategy.

## 8. Why cash is a valid trading decision
**Verified fact:** Every listed portfolio system has capital permission zero.\
**Interpretation:** Insufficient evidence can make non-deployment the only supported action.\
**Opinion:** “Do nothing” is often the most disciplined output of research.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Future evidence that could change a capital gate.

## 9. Missing fills are not a rounding error
**Verified fact:** The guide treats forward scanning for missing fills as a failure mode.\
**Interpretation:** Replacing missing data with future observations can create look-ahead.\
**Opinion:** A missing fill should fail loudly in the record.\
**Unresolved evidence:** Whether a given execution dataset meets its stated contract.
