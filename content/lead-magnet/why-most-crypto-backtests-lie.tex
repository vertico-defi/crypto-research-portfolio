\noindent\textbf{Educational guide.} This publication uses sanitized portfolio evidence. It does not disclose restricted data, private logs, machine paths, unpublished source code, or sensitive implementation details.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Survivorship bias.} Reconstruct the universe as it was known at each decision time.
\item \textbf{Point-in-time universe errors.} Later listings, delistings, and revisions cannot enter earlier decisions.
\item \textbf{Same-bar execution.} A signal cannot use information that was unavailable at the execution price.
\item \textbf{Forward scanning for missing fills.} Future observations cannot repair an unavailable execution price.
\item \textbf{Gap and quarantine leakage.} Use a frozen exclusion rule for unavailable or quarantined records.
\item \textbf{Fold contamination.} Keep tuning and transformations inside their permitted partition.
\item \textbf{Underestimated fees and slippage.} Missing executable prices and unfrozen costs limit interpretation.
\item \textbf{Lucky parameters and random seeds.} Record the search space and distinguish selection from confirmation.
\item \textbf{Confusing test-suite success with profitability.} Tests validate software contracts, not an economic edge.
\item \textbf{Ignoring bootstrap uncertainty.} Point estimates do not communicate sampling variation.
\item \textbf{Ignoring DSR and multiple testing.} More trials raise false-positive risk.
\item \textbf{Opening the final holdout too early.} A holdout seen during development is no longer independent.
\end{enumerate}
