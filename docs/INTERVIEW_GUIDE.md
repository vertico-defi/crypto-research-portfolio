# Interview guide

## Direction V3

**30 seconds:** A leakage-aware cross-sectional forecasting research system in
frozen prospective shadow mode; it produces probabilities, not an accepted
trading return. **Technical:** point-in-time panels, causal labels, rolling
evaluation, and explicit model-identity repairs protect against future leakage.
The key integrity decision was refusing to convert prediction metrics into P&L.

## Perp Carry

**30 seconds:** A cross-venue perpetual-market data reliability system, not a
carry-profit claim. **Technical:** append-only raw data, normalized tables,
clock evidence, health checks, bounded audit lifecycle, and independent
finalization. The difficult bug was scheduler/lifecycle liveness; the valid
next step is audited closure, not a new strategy.

## CTREND

**30 seconds:** A causal Binance USD-M reconstruction that documented a
negative net diagnostic and an integrity failure rather than forcing an edge.
**Technical:** point-in-time universe reconstruction, causal features,
frozen costs, funding accounting, moving-block bootstrap, and gap audits. The
strongest decision was preserving missing funding as missing.

## Control center

**30 seconds:** A read-only registry that normalizes heterogeneous research
states without starting services or treating them as equivalent strategies.

Likely question: *Why publish negative results?* Answer: they demonstrate that
the engineering controls can invalidate an attractive headline before capital
is exposed. Every next step remains constrained by the corresponding evidence
gate.
